常見問題

為什麼有些老屋標示「僅外觀可看」或「狀態不詳」？
收錄的是依法登錄的古蹟與歷史建築，其中很多仍是私人住宅、辦公處所， 或尚未開放參觀。App 會標示開放狀態，請尊重現況，不要進入未開放的建築或打擾住戶。 狀態如有變動，歡迎來信告知。
資料是從哪裡來的？
全部來自文化部文化資產局的公開資料，依「政府資料開放授權條款－第 1 版」釋出。 App 內每一筆資料都標示了來源。若發現與現況不符，通常是原始開放資料尚未更新， 來信告訴我，我會在下次更新時處理。
我的位置資料會被上傳嗎？
不會。定位只在你的裝置上用來計算距離與排序，從不離開裝置。 足跡與收藏存在你自己的 iCloud 私有資料庫，開發者沒有存取權。 詳見隱私權政策。
買了「支持開發」之後廣告還在？
請到「我的」分頁點「回復購買」。若仍未生效，來信附上購買時的 Apple 收據，我會協助處理。
換手機之後，我的足跡與收藏會跟著走嗎？
只要新舊裝置登入同一個 Apple 帳號並開啟 iCloud，資料會自動同步。
可以離線使用嗎？
點位資料存在裝置上，沒有網路也能瀏覽清單與詳細資料。 但地圖圖磚與導航需要連線。

Frequently asked questions

Why are some buildings marked "exterior only" or "status unknown"?
The app catalogs legally designated heritage sites and historic buildings, many of which are still private homes, offices, or simply not open to the public. The app shows each building's access status — please respect it, and don't enter closed buildings or disturb residents. If a status has changed, let me know.
Where does the data come from?
Entirely from the Bureau of Cultural Heritage, Ministry of Culture (Taiwan), released under the Open Government Data License, version 1.0. Every entry in the app credits its source. If something doesn't match reality, it's usually because the source data hasn't been updated yet — email me and I'll address it in the next release.
Is my location data uploaded anywhere?
No. Location is used on your device only, to calculate distances and sort nearby places. It never leaves your device. Check-ins and favorites live in your own private iCloud database, which the developer cannot access. See the privacy policy for details.
I purchased "Support the developer" but ads are still showing.
Go to the Profile tab and tap "Restore Purchases." If that doesn't work, email me with your Apple receipt and I'll sort it out.
Will my check-ins and favorites transfer to a new phone?
Yes — as long as both devices are signed into the same Apple Account with iCloud enabled, your data syncs automatically.
Does it work offline?
Place data is stored on your device, so you can browse listings and details without a connection. Map tiles and directions do require network access.
